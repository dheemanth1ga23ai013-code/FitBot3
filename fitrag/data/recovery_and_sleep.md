# Recovery, Sleep and Soreness
Adults need 7 to 9 hours of sleep per night. Poor sleep reduces recovery, lowers training performance and increases hunger.

Delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS) is the dull ache that appears 12 to 24 hours after unfamiliar or intense exercise and peaks around 24 to 72 hours. It is normal and fades within a few days. Light movement, walking and gentle stretching can ease it.

Take rest days. Most people benefit from at least 1 to 2 rest days per week. Persistent sharp pain, swelling or joint pain is not normal soreness and should be checked by a clinician.
