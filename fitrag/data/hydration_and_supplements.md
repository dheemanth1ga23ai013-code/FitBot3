# Hydration and Supplements
Fluid needs vary by body size, climate and activity. A practical guide is to drink about 400 to 600 ml of water in the 2 hours before exercise and to sip fluids during long or hot sessions. Pale yellow urine is a simple sign of good hydration.

Creatine monohydrate at 3 to 5 grams per day is one of the best-researched supplements for strength and is considered safe for healthy adults. It does not need loading or cycling.

Caffeine at 3 to 6 mg per kg of body weight about an hour before training can improve performance, but avoid it late in the day because it harms sleep.
