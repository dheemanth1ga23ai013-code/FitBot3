# Cardio and Weekly Activity Guidelines
Adults should aim for at least 150 to 300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week, or 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous activity, or an equivalent mix.

Moderate intensity means you can talk but not sing, for example brisk walking or easy cycling. Vigorous intensity means you can only say a few words at a time, for example running or fast swimming.

Adults should also do muscle-strengthening activities that work all major muscle groups on at least 2 days per week.
