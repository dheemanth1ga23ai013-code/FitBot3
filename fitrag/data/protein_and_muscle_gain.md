# Protein and Muscle Gain
Most people who lift weights to build muscle do well with 1.6 to 2.2 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight per day. Going above 2.2 g/kg has not been shown to add meaningful extra muscle.

Spreading protein over 3 to 5 meals, with roughly 20 to 40 grams each, supports muscle protein synthesis. Good sources include eggs, chicken, fish, dairy, lentils, tofu and whey protein.

To gain muscle you generally need a small calorie surplus of about 250 to 500 kcal above maintenance. A larger surplus mostly adds body fat rather than extra muscle.
