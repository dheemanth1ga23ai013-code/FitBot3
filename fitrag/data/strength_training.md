# Strength Training Basics
Progressive overload means gradually increasing the demand on your muscles by adding weight, repetitions or sets over time. It is the main driver of strength and muscle growth.

Beginners can train the whole body 2 to 3 times per week. Compound lifts such as squats, deadlifts, bench press, rows and overhead press work many muscles at once.

A common range for muscle growth is 10 to 20 hard sets per muscle group per week, using loads you can lift for roughly 6 to 15 repetitions close to failure. Good form matters more than heavy weight.
