# Fat Loss and Nutrition
Fat loss requires a sustained calorie deficit. A moderate deficit of 300 to 500 kcal per day is usually sustainable and leads to about 0.25 to 0.5 kg of loss per week.

Keep protein high during a deficit to preserve muscle. Fibre-rich foods such as vegetables, fruit, oats and pulses increase fullness. Extreme crash diets often lead to muscle loss and rebound weight gain.

Weighing yourself at the same time each morning and tracking the weekly average is more reliable than reacting to day-to-day changes caused by water and food volume.
